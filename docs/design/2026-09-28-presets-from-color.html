Presets from Color

How to get from one color@1 clip to each color preset that ships today. Each arrow is one change. Nothing that the old forms can do is lost.

Rules

  • Every number can take a source. Every, life, width, offset, coverage, scale, speed, contrast, brightness and alpha.
  • Choices are settings. Axis, span, plane, grain, boundary, "width relative" and the curve points. These do not animate, the same as today.
  • Time is events. An event starts every every beats and lives for life beats. The curve plays over the life of one event. Every = 0 is one event over the whole clip (today's Time source).
  • Events can overlap. When life is longer than every, several events are live at once. The input is evaluated once per live event, and brightness takes the maximum. This gives several strokes at once and Shimmer.
  • Random picks per event. Random selects a share (coverage) of the units again for each event of the Time source in the same input.

What changes from today

  • color.sparkle@1 goes: Sparkle = brightness from Random × Time. Its every and duration become the Time source's every and life.
  • color.noise@1 goes: Noise is a source on brightness or color. Contrast stays a number input of the Noise source.
  • The Space move block goes: travel is the life of the offset's Time source, path is its curve, width and boundary stay on Space.
  • The Hit source and the form's every go: they are Time with every and life on the source. Two inputs that must stay in step use the same every.
  • Grain moves from Sparkle to Space, Random and Noise.

Open: when color itself takes overlapping events, which event wins. Today color uses the form's events too; the proposal is the newest live event.
