---
title: Lasers
description: "Laser projectors through a DAC: set them up, aim the window, and turn output on safely."
---

Luma draws beam shows for laser projectors driven through an
[Ether Dream](https://ether-dream.com) DAC. A laser clip says which figures
to draw, where and in what color. Luma works out the points for each
projector.

DMX party lasers are ordinary fixtures and need none of this.

## Projectors

Add a **Laser Projector** in Rig like any fixture. It has no DMX address.
Rig's **Lasers** tab then asks what your projectors are: a **Preset** (1, 3,
5 or 10 W RGB) to start from, the **Scan angle**, the **Scanner** speed and
a **Power cap**.

Each projector has a row of its own:

- **DAC**: the box on the network that plays it. With none, it draws on
  screen only.
- **Limit**: an outline in its scan. Light outside it is blanked.
- **Test**: draws the window's edge and a cross, at a quarter of the capped
  power.

## The window

A venue has one **window**: the range of directions its lasers draw in, for
example 5° to 25° up and 20° either side. Every projector shares it, so six
projectors draw six parallel fans. Drag it on the Lasers tab, or ask the
venue's chat to set it.

## Laser clips

A laser clip draws **figures**: beams, lines, rings, polygons and grids.
Start from a laser preset such as Beam fan, Ring or Liquid sky in **Add
pattern**. Laser clips either add to the ones under them or morph them into
their own shapes.

The stage shows the beams as a projector would draw them, corners rounded by
the scanner and scattered in the haze.

## Output

Choose each projector's DAC, then turn on **Laser output** at the top of the
Lasers tab. It is off every time Luma starts, and the status bar says when
it is on.

A projector's row shows how many times a second it draws the picture. Under
25 it flickers: the clip is asking for more than the scanner can draw.

You are responsible for running lasers safely and legally. Set each
projector's limit and power cap before turning output on, and keep the
projector's own interlock and emergency stop in reach.
