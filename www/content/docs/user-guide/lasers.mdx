# Lasers

Luma draws beam shows for laser projectors driven through a DAC. A laser clip never names
points or mirror angles. It says what figures to draw, where and in what
color, and a solver turns that into each projector's point stream, the way an
aim becomes pan and tilt.

DMX party lasers are not covered here. They are ordinary fixtures and play
like any other light.

## Projectors

Add a **Laser Projector** in Rig like any other fixture. A projector has no
DMX address and is not in the patch. It scans around the direction it is
mounted in, out of the glass on its front.

Most rigs hang several of one projector, so Rig's **Lasers** tab asks once
what they are, under **Projectors**:

- **Preset**: 1, 3, 5 or 10 W RGB, a place to start.
- **Scan angle**, **Scanner** speed in kpps, and a **Power cap**.
- Under **Details**: each diode's power, wavelength and threshold, analog or
  on-or-off (TTL) modulation, and the beam's width and divergence.

Drag a number to change it a step at a time, or click it to type one.

Each projector then has its own row:

- **DAC**: the box on the network that plays it. With none, it draws on
  screen only.
- **Test**: draws the window's edge and a cross at its middle, in the room
  and on its DAC.
- Under **Setup**, **DAC output**: mirror, size, offset and swapped axes,
  for a box hung upside down, behind a screen, or wired with its axes
  crossed. These change what the DAC is sent, never the picture in Luma.
- **Limit**: an outline in the projector's own scan. Light outside it is
  blanked. Drag its corners; right-click removes one.
- **A different kind of projector from the rest** gives it settings of its
  own.

## Window

A venue has one **window**: a range of directions in the room, for example
5° to 25° up and 20° either side, shared by every projector. A laser clip
draws on a canvas from −1 to 1 that maps onto it, so the middle of the canvas
is the same direction from every projector: six projectors draw six parallel
fans.

Until you set it, the window is fitted to the projectors: ahead into the
house, just above level. On the Lasers tab it sits on a graph of directions:
drag it to move it, pull a handle to resize it, or drag on the graph to draw
a new one. It lands on whole degrees and is drawn to the middle and to level
when it comes near; Alt resizes about its middle. The grey outlines are
where each projector can reach. You can also ask the venue's chat: its Python has `luma.venue.laser_window(turn=[min, max], tilt=[min, max])`,
in degrees.

## Laser Clips

A laser clip's output is a **laser** node. It draws one or more **figure**
nodes, in order, and holds the clip's alpha. A figure has a kind
(`beams`, `line`, `ring`, `polygon` or `grid`), a count, and the place, size,
rotation and color of each of its elements. The elements are the figure's
heads, so Space, Random, Shuffle and Mirror spread over them as they spread
over a rig's heads.

An **along** node gives 0 to 1 within one element, from a line's start to its
end or once round a ring. A figure input that reads it varies along the
element: a gradient, a bend, or dashes where the color is black.

Laser clips stack in one of two blend modes. **Add** draws both. **Replace**
morphs the elements under the clip into its own, by its alpha. Colors never
blend, and a color clip never reaches a projector.

The laser presets include Beam fan, Beam circle, Beam grid, Sheet, Ring,
Liquid sky, Settle, Soft sheet and Sheet and beams; see the [preset reference](/docs/node-reference/presets). The
[laser node reference](/docs/node-reference/lasers) lists the figure, along
and laser nodes.

## Preview

The visualizer draws the frames the solver makes: the same points a projector
would be sent. It runs them through a model of the scanner's mirrors, so a
fast shape's corners round and dwell brighter, and it scatters the beams in
the venue's haze.

## Output

Luma plays a projector through an [Ether Dream](https://ether-dream.com) DAC
on the same network. Choose the DAC on the projector's row, then turn on
**Laser output** at the top of the Lasers tab.

- Laser output is off every time Luma starts. While it is on, the status bar
  says so, and pressing that turns it off.
- **Test** draws the window's edge and a cross at its middle on one
  projector, at a quarter of its capped power, in the room as on the DAC.
- Max Brightness and the Perform master dim lasers with the lights, and a
  blackout blanks them.
- A projector's row says how many times a second it draws the picture. Under
  25 it flickers: the clip is drawing more than the scanner can.

You are responsible for running lasers safely and legally. Set each
projector's limit and power cap before turning output on, and keep the
projector's own interlock and emergency stop in reach.
