# Lasers

Luma draws beam shows for ILDA laser projectors. A laser clip never names
points or mirror angles. It says what figures to draw, where and in what
color, and a solver turns that into each projector's point stream, the way an
aim becomes pan and tilt.

DMX party lasers are not covered here. They are ordinary fixtures and play
like any other light.

## Projectors

A projector is a fixture whose definition has the type `Laser Projector`.
Luma ships `Generic/Generic-ILDA-Laser-Projector.qxf`. Place it on the stage
in Rig like any other fixture. Its scan field is the pan and tilt range of its
definition, and it draws 30,000 points a second. It scans around the
direction it is mounted in.

## Zones

A **zone** is a named window of directions in the room, for example 5° to 20°
up and 60° either side. Every projector shares it. A laser clip draws on a
canvas from −1 to 1 that maps onto the zone, so the middle of the canvas is
the same direction from every projector: six projectors draw six parallel
fans.

A laser clip names its zone. With no zone, or a zone the venue does not have,
it uses the venue's first zone. A venue with no zones draws ahead into the
house, inside every projector's scan.

The app has no zone editor yet. Ask the venue's chat to make one: its Python
has `luma.venue.laser_zones()` and
`luma.venue.laser_zone(name, turn=[min, max], tilt=[min, max])`, in degrees.

## Laser Clips

A laser clip's output is a **laser** node. It draws one or more **figure**
nodes, in order, and holds the clip's alpha and its zone. A figure has a kind
(`beams`, `line`, `ring`, `polygon` or `grid`), a count, and the place, size,
rotation and color of each of its elements. The elements are the figure's
heads, so Space, Random, Shuffle and Mirror spread over them as they spread
over a rig's heads.

An **along** node gives 0 to 1 within one element, from a line's start to its
end or once round a ring. A figure input that reads it varies along the
element: a gradient, a bend, or dashes where the color is black.

Laser clips stack in one of two blend modes. **Add** draws both. **Replace**
morphs the elements under the clip into its own, by its alpha. Colors never
blend, and a color clip never reaches a projector.

The laser presets include Beam fan, Beam circle, Beam grid, Sheet, Ring,
Liquid sky, Settle, Soft sheet and Sheet and beams; see the [preset reference](/docs/node-reference/presets). The
[laser node reference](/docs/node-reference/lasers) lists the figure, along
and laser nodes.

## Preview

The visualizer draws the frames the solver makes: the same points a projector
would be sent. It runs them through a model of the scanner's mirrors, so a
fast shape's corners round and dwell brighter, and it scatters the beams in
the venue's haze.

Luma does not send laser frames to projector hardware yet. Laser shows play
in the visualizer and in exported videos only.
