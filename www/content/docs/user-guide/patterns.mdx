# Forms and Presets

A clip plays one **form**. A form is a shipped graph with a fixed set of inputs. The clip holds a value for every input of its form. A named look, such as Chase or Rainbow, is a **preset**: saved input values of one form.

## Forms

| Form | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| `color@1` | One color. Color and brightness are fixed, over time, per hit, across space, or a stroke that travels across space (a chase) |
| `color.sparkle@1` | Each event lights a random share of the heads |
| `color.noise@1` | Soft brightness that wanders across space and time |
| `strobe.constant@1` | Fixture shutter strobe at Rate × Alpha |
| `aim@1` | Points moving heads: a base aim, a fan across the axis and a motion shape |

The id of a form never changes meaning. A new meaning is a new version.

Every form has an **Alpha** input. Alpha is how much the clip counts. For light it multiplies the clip's brightness. For aim in Replace blend it is the blend toward the clip's aim. For aim in Offset blend it scales the fan and motion degrees.

## Input Values

An input takes a fixed value, or a **source** where the form allows it:

| Source | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Fixed | One value, on all heads, for the whole clip |
| Over time | One curve over the whole clip, all heads equal |
| Per hit | One curve over the life of each hit |
| Noise | Smooth random wandering over time |
| Audio | The energy of one frequency range of the track's full mix |
| Space | Values along an axis of the heads: a gradient for a color, a curve for brightness |

On a color, Over time and Per hit can read a gradient. A curve gives the position in the gradient over the clip or over each hit. This is a color fade; with **Every** set, it repeats every few beats, as in Rainbow.

## Colors

Luma keeps colors in Rec. 2020, a wider range than a screen's sRGB. LED fixtures can make many colors outside sRGB: deep red, cyan, lime and royal blue. The color picker reaches all of Rec. 2020. The hex field is sRGB. When a color is outside sRGB, the field shows the nearest sRGB color and says **Outside sRGB**.

A fixture or a screen that cannot make a color shows the nearest color it can make. That color has the same hue and lightness, with less saturation.

## Space and chases

A Space source has an **axis**: the source (X, Y, Z, order, radial, angle or random), the span (the selection, each fixture or each group), an optional mirror, and a plane for radial and angle. Each head reads the gradient or the curve at its position on the axis. The Gradient preset is a Space gradient on color.

Brightness can also **move**. Then its curve is one stroke, from the tail (0) to the head (1), and the stroke travels along the axis once per hit of **Every**. Heads outside a stroke are dark. The movement sets:

- **Path**: where the stroke is over its life. A backward path runs the other way.
- **Travel**: beats for one stroke to cross the axis. 0 is the whole clip.
- **Width**: the size of the stroke, a share of the axis or of the gap between strokes.
- **Boundary**: Clip (a stroke enters and leaves fully) or Wrap.

Chase, Wave, Ripple, Spin, Bounce, Alternating sides, Stepped chase and Grow are presets of a moving brightness. While brightness moves, Per hit sources on the other inputs follow each stroke.

## Add a Clip

Open the picker in the track editor. It lists the presets. Choose one. Luma copies its values into a new clip. Edit the inputs in the clip inspector.

Layers combine forms. Often one clip is enough: for a rainbow that chases, give color a gradient over time and give brightness a moving Space source.

The [node reference](/docs/node-reference/forms) shows the inputs of each form.
