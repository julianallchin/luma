# The Window

A Luma window shows one venue. The title bar reads `venue / song / score` at the
left, each name a button, and holds the mode switch at the right. Under it, one page holds the agent panel on
the left and the mode's stage and body beside it. The status bar under the
page shows sync, DMX output, imports and updates, and what the mode is doing.

![Arrange mode: the agent panel on the left, the lit stage with the song and score menu at its top, and the score's clips in lanes under the song's waveform.](/screenshots/arrange.webp)

## Modes

A venue is worked on in three modes. Switch with the mode switch, or with the
keys `1`, `2` and `3` when no text field has the keyboard.

| Mode | Key | What it shows |
|------|-----|---------------|
| **Rig** | `1` | The stage builder over the venue page: the patch, the outputs and the groups. See [Define Your Venue](/docs/user-guide/venues). |
| **Arrange** | `2` | The open score's timeline under the stage. See [Place Clips](/docs/user-guide/annotations). |
| **Live** | `3` | The deck panel beside the stage, and the venue's Perform set under it. See [Live](/docs/user-guide/performing). |

Switching mode closes nothing. Playback goes on, a loop stays armed and a chat
keeps working. Each venue remembers its open score, and the next
launch reopens the last venue. Switching venue keeps you on the same page: Rig
stays Rig, Live stays Live, and from the song list or a score you land on the
other venue's song list, with the same song selected if that venue has it. A new venue opens in Rig.

The stage is the venue in 3D, lit by the mode's body. Drag its lower edge to
resize it. **⌘⇧V** hides or shows it, and **⇧F** over the stage makes it fullscreen.

## The Venue Menu

The venue's name at the left of the title bar opens the venue menu. It lists
your venues, with a mark on the ones shared with you. Pick one to switch the
window to it. The menu also has **New venue…**, **Join with code…**, **Share**
for the open venue, **Settings…** and **Sign out** (or **Sign in**).

## The Agent Panel

The agent panel on the left is the chat of the mode's body: the venue page
has the venue's chat, a score its own, and the Perform set its own. In Arrange
with no score open, the panel says so. A mode whose chat is working, or has
finished while you were away, carries a dot on its switch.

**⌘B** hides or shows the panel. Drag its edge to resize it.

## The Song List

The song list is the page of the open venue's songs that a score is back
from. **⌘P**, or clicking the song's name in the title bar, closes the open
score and goes there with its song selected. Choosing a score opens it in the
list's place.

Its head holds the title and count, a search, the **Mine** / **All** filter
and **Add songs**. Under it are three columns:

![The song list: the venue's songs with their cover art, and the selected song's scores in each venue.](/screenshots/songs.webp)

1. **Places.** **All songs**, then the venue's folders, then **New folder**. A
   folder holds links to songs and does not nest. Right-click a folder to
   rename or delete it.
2. **Songs** in the selected place: the cover, the title, the artist and
   tempo, the length and the number of scores. Right-click a song to file it
   in folders.
3. **Scores** of the selected song. This venue's come first, with **New
   score**, then each other venue's under that venue's name. A click opens a
   score. A score of another venue asks "Switch to …?" first, then switches
   the window to that venue.

Up and Down move in a column, Right goes into the next column, Left goes
back, and Enter opens the score under the cursor. A search lists matches from
every place. The list remembers the folder and song it was left on.

The song list is a page of its own, without the agent panel. Rig, Live and
an open score show the panel as you last left it, open or hidden.

## The Song and Score

In Arrange, the open song and score follow the venue in the title bar. Click
the song to close the score and go back to the song list, with that song
selected. The score shows its
name, or its number when it has none. Click it to list every score of the
song, this venue's first, with **New score** at the end of this venue's, and
**Rename** and **Duplicate** for the open score below them. A score in another venue asks before the window
switches to it.

## Escape

Escape closes the innermost thing that is open: a menu, the settings page, a
dialog, the clip graph page, and last the selection.
