# Welcome to Luma

Luma is a native desktop application for lighting control. A light show in Luma does not name DMX channels. It names groups of fixtures and musical events. Each venue maps those names to its own fixtures.

## How It Works

Luma keeps two kinds of data apart:

1. **Your library.** Tracks, scores, clips and pattern graphs. This data does not refer to DMX addresses.
2. **Venues.** A venue records the fixtures in one room, their DMX addresses, their positions and their groups.

A score belongs to one track and one venue. Its clips select fixtures by group name. When the score plays, Luma resolves the group names against the venue.

A window shows one venue in one of three modes: **Rig** builds the room, **Arrange** edits a score on its track's timeline, and **Live** follows the DJ and plays the venue's live patterns.
