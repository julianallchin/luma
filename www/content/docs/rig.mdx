title: Rig
description: "Build a venue once: the stage, the fixtures, the patch and the groups that scores aim at."
---

A venue is one physical room: its fixtures, their DMX addresses, where they
hang, and the groups they belong to. Rig (key `1`) is where you build it.
Everything else in Luma is written against groups, so this is the only part
tied to a room.

Ask the venue's chat to do any of this for you, from "hang eight movers on
the back truss" to a whole rig from a description.

## The stage

- **A** opens the add dialog: **Fixtures** or **Stage pieces**. Tab switches
  between them.
- Place a piece, such as a truss. Then choose a fixture and click the truss
  to lay a row along it, or click the floor to stand one light there.
- **W** moves and **E** rotates. **⌘D** duplicates. **F** frames the
  selection. **⌘Z** undoes.
- Deleting a piece deletes the lights on it.

Positions matter. A chase travels across a group by where its lights really
are, so the same clip works on 8 fixtures or 80.

## The patch

Every light you place gets a row in the patch table. Set its **Mode** to the
mode set on the real fixture, then press **Auto patch** to address the lot.
Addresses you set by hand are kept.

Switch the page from **Fixtures** to **Patch** to see each universe as a grid
and drag fixtures to new addresses.

## Groups

A score never names a fixture. It names groups, such as `front_wash` or
`back_movers`, and plays on whatever is in them.

- **Generate from stage** makes a starting set by role and position.
- **New group** makes one from the fixtures you have selected.
- Renaming a group breaks nothing. Deleting one leaves the clips that aimed
  at it lighting nothing, and Luma tells you how many there are first.

Names are snake_case, and `all` is taken: it means every fixture. Use the
same names in every venue, so you and the agent ask for the same thing
everywhere.

## Output

Luma sends DMX to Art-Net nodes and to the SoundSwitch Micro DMX Interface
and Control One.

1. On the venue page, open **Output** and give each universe a port with
   **Add output**. A universe with no output is not sent.
2. Turn on **Enable DMX Output** in **Settings → DMX Output**. **Max
   Brightness** there caps everything.

## Sharing

The venue's name in the title bar opens the venue menu. **Share** makes an
invite code, and **Join with code…** takes one. Anyone with the code can
open and edit the venue.

A score belongs to whoever made it. Everyone else can play it, and
**Duplicate** it to get a copy of their own.

## The view

The camera button on the stage opens **View settings**: indoor or outdoor,
haze, the grid and the render scale. Haze and environment belong to the
venue. The rest belongs to this computer.
