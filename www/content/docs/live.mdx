Live (key `3`) follows the DJ and plays each song's score as it is mixed in.
Under the stage is the venue's **Perform set**: pages of pads and faders for
everything a score did not plan.

## Decks

**Add source** in the deck panel connects one:

| Source | Follows | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Pro DJ Link** | Pioneer CDJs | Luma takes a player number. Give it one no real deck uses. |
| **StageLinQ** | Denon DJ and Engine DJ | Nothing to set up. |
| **Rekordbox** | rekordbox on this computer | Through [rkbx_link](https://github.com/grufkork/rkbx_link). Turn on `osc.msg.n/time` in its config, and load tracks after Luma is listening. |
| **Virtual** | Luma's own player | For practising. **Choose song** plays any song in your library. |

Luma matches what each deck loads against your library and plays that song's
score. Two decks blend by their volume. With no deck, the Perform set runs
on a tapped tempo.

## The Perform set

A pad turns a pattern on and off, or flashes it while held. Beside the pads
sit group cells, color lists, faders, tap tempo and the master.

- **Tap a group, then a pad**, and the pattern plays on that group. With no
  group chosen, it plays where it was made to.
- **Tap a color** to set the color under everything.
- **Clear** chooses nothing. Hold it to let every effect go.

**Edit layout** arranges the grid. In it, **Busk page** builds a whole page
from your rig: groups, colors, effects, movement, strobes and the master. Or
ask Live's chat for the page you want.

## Controllers

Any MIDI device, and anything that sends OSC to UDP port 8000, can play the
set. In **Edit layout**, select a cell, press **Learn**, and move the fader
or press the key.

For a controller with pad lights, press **Set up with the agent**. The chat
lights the pads one at a time, asks what you see, and writes the mapping.

## Going live

Turn on **Enable DMX Output** in **Settings → DMX Output**. It is the same
switch [Rig](/docs/rig#output) describes, and **Max Brightness** beside it
caps the whole rig.
