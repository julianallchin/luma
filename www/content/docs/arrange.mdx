Arrange (key `2`) is a song's timeline under the stage. You place **clips**
on it. The clips of one song in one venue are a **score**.

The fastest way to a score is to ask for one. The score's chat listens to
the song, writes the clips and explains its choices. Then ask for changes:
"hold the big hit for the second drop", "less strobe in the verse".

![Arrange: the agent's notes on a score at the left, the lit stage, and the score's clips in lanes under the song's waveform.](/screenshots/arrange.webp)

## Songs

**⌘P** opens the song list: folders, songs, and each song's scores. **Add
songs** adds a song from your library to this venue, and **Import tracks**
brings in new ones from Rekordbox, Engine DJ, files or a folder. A song can
be up to 10 minutes long.

Luma analyzes each song in the background: beats and bars, stems, chords and
drum hits. The status bar shows progress.

## Scores

A score belongs to one song and one venue, and a song can have many. **New
score** is in the song list and in the score menu in the title bar. A score
is its author's to edit. Anyone else in the venue can play it or duplicate
it.

## Clips

**Add pattern**, or a right-click on the timeline, opens the pattern list
with a live preview of each preset on your venue. The clip lands at the
playhead, or fills the span you selected.

A clip has:

- a **selection**: `all`, one group, or several, written `front_wash | back_movers`
- a start and a length, in beats
- a **blend mode**, which says how it combines with the clips under it
- its own **graph**, which says what it does

Clips stack in lanes. A color clip changes only color, an aim clip only
where movers point, a strobe clip only strobe, so the three layer freely.

## The graph

Double-click a clip to open its graph: a few node cards wired left to right
into one output. Each input has a chip that says where its value comes from.
Change **Value** to **Over time**, **Over space**, **Audio** or **Random**
and Luma wires in the node for you.

A preset is only a starting graph. Once it is in a clip, it is that clip's to
change. The [node reference](/docs/node-reference) lists every node and
preset.

## Playing and exporting

**Space** plays and pauses. **F** follows the playhead, **H** fits every
lane, and **⌘L** loops the selection. **Export show** records the score as a
video.
